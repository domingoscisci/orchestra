# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Orchestra to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work and considerate about the person.
- Give and accept technical criticism; explain the reason, not just the verdict.
- Assume a question comes from missing context, not from bad faith.
- Respect that maintainers decide scope, and that an unanswered request has not
  been agreed to.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Dismissing a report of a problem because the reporter is not a maintainer.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any
space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **security.regain404@silomails.com**. Reports
are read by the maintainer, kept confidential, and answered within 7 days. A
report about the maintainer themselves may be sent through the same address;
if that is not workable, say so in the report and the answer will state what
can be done.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and repetition, the response is one of: a private
correction, a request to stop the specific behavior, a temporary ban from
participation, or a permanent ban. The maintainer records what was decided and
tells the reporter the outcome.

## Attribution

This code follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and is
written for a single-maintainer project rather than a foundation-backed one.
